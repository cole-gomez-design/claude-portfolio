About me

I'm a web designer and digital experience specialist with a background in higher education, educational technology, and digital content development. My work focuses on creating accessible, user-friendly websites and digital platforms that make information easier to navigate and use.

I've worked on website design, development, and UX improvements across the University of Florida, including projects for the College of Medicine and Office of Real Estate. I'm particularly interested in the intersection of web design, user experience, and educational technology, and how thoughtful digital design can improve access to information and learning.
